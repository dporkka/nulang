## Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-25_

### 1. Information We Collect

**Nulang.org (documentation site):**
We do not collect personal information. The documentation site is a static website hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect anonymized analytics (see Third-Party Services below).

**Nulang Cloud (hosted platform):**
- **Account information**: email address, name (if provided) when you create an account.
- **Usage data**: program execution metrics, resource consumption, error logs.
- **User content**: the Nulang source code and data you deploy to the platform.

### 2. How We Use Information

- To provide, maintain, and improve the Services.
- To communicate with you about your account and the Services.
- To monitor and prevent abuse of the Services.

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

### 3. Data Storage and Security

Nulang Cloud stores your data on Cloudflare's infrastructure. We implement reasonable security measures but cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 4. Cookies

Nulang.org does not set cookies. Nulang Cloud may use essential cookies for authentication and session management.

### 5. Third-Party Services

- **Cloudflare Pages**: Hosts Nulang.org. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- **Stripe**: Processes payments for Nulang Cloud subscriptions and donations. See [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).
- **OpenAI / Ollama**: LLM inference providers used by Nulang Cloud's AI runtime. Your prompts are sent to these providers according to their respective privacy policies.

### 6. Data Retention

We retain your account information and deployed code for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting support.

### 7. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

### 8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify users of material changes via email or a notice on the website.

### 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact [support@nulang.org](mailto:support@nulang.org).
